# Slide 3 — Story of the Game

## Plot Summary

### Points for the Slide

- The story takes place during the French Revolution.
- Arno Dorian loses his father when he is a child.
- Later, his adoptive father is murdered.
- Arno joins the Assassin Brotherhood.
- He searches for the people responsible for the murders.
- Throughout the game, he fights the Templars and uncovers conspiracies.

### Speech:

The story of Assassin’s Creed Unity takes place during the French Revolution in Paris. The main character, Arno Dorian, loses his father at a young age and is later adopted by another family. However, tragedy happens again when his adoptive father is murdered.

Arno is blamed for the crime and eventually joins the Assassin Brotherhood. During the game, he tries to discover the truth behind the murders and stop the Templars, who seek power and control. The story is full of action, betrayal, and emotional moments. It also shows many historical events and famous places from revolutionary France.

# Slide 6 — How the Game Works

## Gameplay

### Points for the Slide

- Explore Paris freely
    
- Complete story missions
    
- Use stealth and parkour
    
- Fight enemies with swords and weapons
    
- Solve mysteries and side quests
    

### To Progress You Should:

- Complete missions
    
- Upgrade weapons and armor
    
- Earn experience points

### Speech:

The gameplay in Assassin’s Creed Unity is very interesting and dynamic. Players can freely explore the city of Paris and complete different missions. The main goal is to follow the story and uncover secrets connected to Arno’s past.

One of the most important mechanics in the game is parkour. Players can climb buildings, jump across rooftops, and move quickly through the city. The game also focuses heavily on stealth. Often players must hide from enemies, move quietly, and attack without being noticed.

To progress in the game, players complete missions and earn experience points. These points help unlock new skills and abilities. Players can also buy stronger weapons and better equipment. Besides the main story, there are many side activities such as solving crimes, finding treasures, and helping citizens.
